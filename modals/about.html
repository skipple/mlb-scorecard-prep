About

I'm a baseball fan who loves keeping score. Before every game, I'd find myself bouncing between a handful of websites fill out my scorecard. Lineups here, team stats there, and umpires... whenever MLB felt like announcing them.

So I built Scorecard Prep. Everything one needs to fill out the score card before first pitch in one place.

Suggestions and issues can be submitted through GitHub.

Data comes from the MLB Stats API.
Field outlines come from Baseball Savant.
